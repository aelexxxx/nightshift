# X / Twitter growth

You get a handful of posts per day (hard cap). Treat each like a product
release: small, sharp, useful.

## What earns attention (pick per post, don't mix)

- **Specific lesson**: one concrete thing learned, with the number or
  screenshot that proves it. Build-in-public is this company's native genre —
  real KPIs, real failures, real fixes.
- **Strong opinion**: a defensible position on the niche, stated plainly.
- **Useful artifact**: checklist, snippet, before/after, tiny tool.
- **Story**: something that happened, told in 2–4 short beats with a real
  detail. No fables, no invented anecdotes.

## Hooks (first line = 80% of the work)

- State the surprising fact or result first: "We deleted 40% of our landing
  page. Signups went up."
- Numbers beat adjectives; outcomes beat topics.
- Banned: "🧵 Thread:", "Hot take:", "Unpopular opinion:", "I can't believe
  more people aren't talking about this", engagement-bait questions, "RT if".

## Threads (post_thread)

- Only when one idea genuinely needs buildup — 4–8 tweets, each standalone
  readable, each under 280 with room to breathe.
- First tweet must work alone; last tweet may contain the single CTA/link.
- One thread beats five filler tweets. When in doubt, fewer, better.

## Cadence & hygiene

- Prefer 1–3 good posts to hitting the cap. Check outbox/posted/ first —
  never repeat yourself, do build on what performed (note results in
  memory/LESSONS.md).
- Reply to genuine replies helpfully (that's free distribution); never
  reply-guy strangers at scale, never auto-DM, never follow-churn — these
  get accounts suspended and are banned here anyway.
- Every post passes the copywriting de-AI pass. AI-flavored posts are
  actively ratio'd in 2026; one generic post costs more credibility than
  ten silent days.
- Log what you can observe: posts published, replies received, profile
  clicks if visible, follower count when checkable (log_kpi).

## The weekly question

At the strategy review: which single format got real engagement this week?
Double down on that; kill what flopped 3× (encode both in overrides).
