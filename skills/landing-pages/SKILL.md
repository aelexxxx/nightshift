# Landing pages

A landing page has one job: move one visitor segment to one action. Every
element either serves that or gets cut.

## Section order (default skeleton)

1. **Headline** — the sharpest *specific outcome*, not the category.
   "Ship your changelog in 2 minutes" beats "The modern changelog platform".
   Formula options: outcome + timeframe; pain inverted; the blunt claim you
   can prove. Subhead: one sentence of mechanism ("how").
2. **Primary CTA** above the fold, exactly one verb ("Start free", "Get the
   checklist"). Repeat the same CTA at bottom; nothing else competes.
3. **Show the product in the first screen** — screenshot, 20-second demo
   loop, or live widget. If there's nothing to show, show the artifact of
   the outcome (the report it makes, the inbox at zero).
4. **Problem block** — 2–3 sentences in the customer's own words (steal from
   AUDIENCE.md). The reader must think "that's literally me".
5. **How it works** — 3 steps max, each one line + visual. Mechanism creates
   belief.
6. **Proof** — real quotes with names/handles, numbers, logos. Pre-traction:
   founder's build-in-public note beats fake logos. NEVER invent proof.
7. **Objection handling** — a short FAQ answering the top 3 real objections
   (from AUDIENCE.md "Objections heard"), including price and "what if it
   doesn't work for my case".
8. **Final CTA** + risk reversal (free tier, refund policy, "cancel anytime"
   — only what's actually true).

## Rules

- One page, one audience. Two audiences → two pages.
- Every claim passes the copywriting skill's de-AI pass; landing pages are
  where "seamless, robust, all-in-one" goes to die.
- No sliders/carousels, no stock photos of people laughing at laptops, no
  feature grids with 12 icons. Cut until it hurts.
- Page speed and mobile first — test with the engineering agent before
  calling it shipped.
- Measure: unique visits, CTA clicks, signups (log_kpi each night when
  observable). Change ONE section per experiment (see experiment-design).

## Checklist before shipping

- [ ] Headline states a specific outcome a stranger understands in 3 s
- [ ] CTA visible without scrolling; the same single action everywhere
- [ ] Product (or outcome artifact) visible in first screen
- [ ] At least one piece of true, verifiable proof
- [ ] De-AI pass done on all copy
- [ ] Loads fast on mobile; no console errors
- [ ] Analytics/KPI counting confirmed working
