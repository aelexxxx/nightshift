# Launching

A launch is a repeatable event, not a one-shot. Small products should launch
something every few weeks: the product, a feature, a free tool, a dataset,
a teardown.

## Before anything ships publicly

- The landing page passes the landing-pages checklist, signup flow tested
  end-to-end by the engineering agent, analytics counting.
- One sentence exists that explains the product to a stranger in 3 seconds.
  If that sentence isn't sharp, the launch will fail regardless of channel.
- A "proof artifact" exists to show, not tell: demo GIF, live example,
  before/after.

## Channel playbook (cheapest first)

- **Niche communities** (subreddits, Discords, forums where AUDIENCE.md says
  the customer lives): highest conversion, strictest norms. Give first —
  answer questions for days before any link. Post as a story or a useful
  writeup, never as an ad. Read each community's self-promo rules (WebSearch)
  and respect them to the letter; getting banned burns the channel forever.
- **Directories & lists** (alternativeto, niche tool lists, awesome-lists via
  GitHub PR): one-time effort, permanent trickle. Batch-submit in one night.
- **Product Hunt**: worth one focused launch. Needs: crisp tagline, first
  comment from the maker telling the origin story honestly, gallery that
  shows the product working. Launch Tue–Thu. Don't beg for upvotes; do tell
  your existing audience it's live.
- **Hacker News (Show HN)**: brutal and honest. Title = "Show HN: X – plain
  description". No marketing language whatsoever (the de-AI pass at maximum
  strength); technical detail in the first comment; stay online to answer
  everything fast. A flopped Show HN is fine; a defensive maker is fatal.
- **X**: build-in-public thread on launch day (twitter-growth skill), then
  keep shipping publicly.

## Sequencing (spread over nights)

1. Night 1: directories + prep posts for each community in its native tone.
2. Night 2: community posts (max 1–2 communities per night — feedback loops
   matter more than blast radius).
3. Night 3+: PH/HN when the pitch has survived contact with real users.
4. Every launch night: inbox and replies checked next run without fail —
   the launch IS the conversations it starts.

## Measure & learn

Log per channel: visits, signups, replies/comments (log_kpi with the channel
in the note). At the weekly review, rank channels by signups-per-effort and
encode the ranking in overrides. Failed launches produce the best LESSONS.md
entries — write down *why* (wrong channel? weak sentence? no proof?).
