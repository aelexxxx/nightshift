# SEO & long-form content

SEO is the slowest channel here (months, not nights) but compounds while the
others reset daily. Play it as a background loop, not tonight's hero project.

## Topic selection (where the game is won)

- Target queries with clear intent and weak competition: "how to X with Y",
  comparisons, error messages, "X alternative", niche how-tos. Head terms
  are unwinnable at domain authority zero — don't try.
- Source topics from reality: inbound questions, AUDIENCE.md verbatims,
  forum/Reddit threads found via WebSearch, your own build problems.
- One page = one query intent. Check the current SERP (WebSearch) first:
  if the top results are thin, outdated, or generic AI sludge, that's the
  opening.

## Structure (answer-first, always)

1. H1 = the query, phrased naturally.
2. First paragraph answers the question completely in 2–4 sentences. No
   warm-up, no "in this article we will". Readers and search engines both
   reward this; it's also what gets quoted by AI answer engines.
3. Then expand: steps with real code/screenshots/numbers, edge cases,
   honest limitations, FAQ from real objections.
4. Internal links to 2–3 related pages (build topic clusters over weeks);
   one CTA at the end, relevant to the query's intent.

## Quality bar

- The de-AI pass (copywriting skill) applies double: search engines and
  readers now both discount AI-flavored sludge. Include something that
  can't be generated: your own data, a real benchmark, an original
  screenshot, a tested edge case.
- 800 solid words beat 2500 padded ones. No keyword stuffing — write the
  words humans use, synonyms happen naturally.
- Every page needs: title tag = claim + query, meta description that earns
  the click, fast load (engineering agent verifies).

## Rhythm & measurement

- Sustainable pace: 1–2 pieces per week, each genuinely useful. Ship, index
  (submit sitemap), interlink, move on.
- Log: pages published, impressions/clicks once Search Console exists
  (escalate setup to owner as a "Needs from you"), inbound signups with
  content source when trackable.
- Review at the weekly strategy pass: which topics get impressions →
  cluster deeper there. Zero impressions after 6 weeks → topic thesis was
  wrong, note it in LESSONS.md.
