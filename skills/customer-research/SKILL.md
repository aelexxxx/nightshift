# Customer research (async, zero meetings)

An agent can't do coffee chats, but most founder research is desk research
done honestly. The output is always the same: dated entries in AUDIENCE.md
(segments, verbatim language, objections) that every other skill feeds on.

## Methods, ranked by signal

1. **Reply mining (own inbox)** — the highest-signal source you have. Every
   inbound email contains the customer's words for their problem, their
   objection, or their moment of delight. Extract verbatims into AUDIENCE.md
   every run; this costs minutes and compounds forever.
2. **Review mining (competitors)** — read 1–3-star AND 5-star reviews of
   competing tools (G2, app stores, Amazon, Trustpilot via WebSearch/
   WebFetch). 1–3 stars = unmet needs and switching triggers; 5 stars = the
   job-to-be-done in the customer's words. Copy exact phrases, with source
   and date.
3. **Community listening** — the subreddits/forums/Discords from AUDIENCE.md.
   Search for the problem space, not the product name. Collect: how they
   phrase the problem, what they've tried, what they refuse to pay for.
4. **Search listening** — autocomplete, "people also ask", forum questions
   (via WebSearch). Reveals the vocabulary for seo-content topics.
5. **Micro-surveys** — a single question in a reply or post ("what did you
   try before this?"). One question, genuinely curious, never a form.

## Rules

- **Verbatims are sacred.** Record exact words in quotes with source+date.
  Paraphrasing launders out the gold — the copy skills need the raw phrasing.
- Separate observation from interpretation: "3 reviews complain setup takes
  hours" (fact) vs "onboarding is our wedge" (hypothesis → experiment-design).
- Look for patterns of 3+ before acting; a single loud datapoint is an
  anecdote.
- Date everything; re-validate entries older than ~6 months before building
  on them.
- Never fabricate research. An empty AUDIENCE.md section is a to-do, not a
  fill-in-the-blank.

## Cadence

- Every run: mine whatever replies came in.
- Weekly (strategy review): one deliberate research block on the leakiest
  funnel stage — e.g. signups stall → mine objections; no traffic → mine
  vocabulary for topics.
- Each block ends with: 3–10 new AUDIENCE.md lines, and (if warranted) one
  proposed experiment.
