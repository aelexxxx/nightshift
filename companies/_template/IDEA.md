# Business Brief

The CEO agent reads this first, every night. The more specific you are here,
the less generic the output. Replace every section.

## The idea

What does this company sell, to whom, and why would they pay? One paragraph,
no buzzwords.

## The customer

Who exactly? Where do they hang out online? What words do they use for their
problem?

## Current status

Greenfield? Existing product? Existing audience? List URLs, repos, accounts
that already exist.

## Business model & pricing

How does money come in? What's the price point hypothesis?

## Constraints

Things the agent must respect: tech stack preferences, topics to avoid,
positioning red lines, jurisdictions, existing commitments.

## Definition of success

What should be true in 30 / 90 days? Be numeric where possible — these become
the KPIs that drive nightly priorities.
